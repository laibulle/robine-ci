# Robine visual identity

## Primary mark

The Robine mark is the geometric `R` stored at `priv/static/images/brand/robine-mark.png`.
Its navy silhouette is the primary signal. The teal plane adds continuity with the product palette.

The husky is a restrained negative-space detail, not a standalone mascot. It must never be enlarged,
animated, recolored, captioned, or repeated independently from the `R`. At navigation and favicon sizes,
the mark must read as an `R` before the animal reference becomes noticeable.

## Product variants

- **Compact:** the mark alone, for favicons and constrained controls.
- **Navigation:** the mark followed by `Robine CI`.
- **Marketing signature:** the mark followed by the Robine wordmark; the husky remains inside the mark.

Use the navy master on light surfaces and `robine-mark-dark.png` on dark surfaces. The dark variant
uses an off-white primary shape and a brighter teal accent; it must switch with the product's explicit
theme rather than relying only on the operating-system preference.

Keep clear space around the mark and do not place it inside an additional colored or rounded container.
